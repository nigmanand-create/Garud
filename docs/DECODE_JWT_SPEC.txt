GarudHub should be positioned as a complete B2B Drone Ecosystem Platform connecting Indian and international companies with buyers, distributors, dealers, service providers, training organizations, enterprises and government opportunities.

7. Core Positioning

GarudHub — India’s B2B Drone Ecosystem

Connect Businesses. Enable Technology. Build Partnerships. Grow in India.

8. Website Objective

The website should communicate that GarudHub is not simply a drone information website. It is a business-generation platform where drone manufacturers, OEMs, international companies, distributors, dealers, service providers, training institutes, technology companies and buyers can connect, list products/services, submit RFQs, generate leads and develop partnerships.
