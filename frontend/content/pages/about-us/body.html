Who We Are

One platform for a fragmented drone economy

As drone technology becomes increasingly important across agriculture, infrastructure, surveying, mapping, photography, construction, education, logistics and other industries, customers often have to approach several different organisations for different requirements — one platform may sell drones, another may offer training, while separate providers handle repairs, insurance or finance.

GarudHub is a specialized digital marketplace built to bring the entire drone community together on one trusted and accessible platform — connecting manufacturers, vendors, sellers, service providers, training institutes, professionals, businesses and customers.

Skills and Careers

Drone training and skill development

Technology becomes truly valuable when people have the knowledge and skills to use it safely and responsibly. GarudHub aims to connect learners, students, working professionals, entrepreneurs and organizations with drone-training opportunities offered by participating training providers.

Training listings may cover introductory drone awareness, basic flying skills, commercial drone operations, agricultural applications, aerial photography, surveying, mapping, inspection, maintenance and other specialized subjects — through classroom sessions, practical workshops or online learning.

Drone Jobs and Career Opportunities

Connecting talent with opportunity

The growth of the drone industry is creating opportunities for pilots, trainers, technicians, engineers, data analysts, sales professionals, project coordinators and other skilled individuals. GarudHub aims to connect capable candidates with businesses and organizations looking for drone-related talent.

Employers and service providers may use the platform to publish vacancies, internships, freelance projects and contractual requirements. Candidates can explore opportunities that match their education, training, certification and professional experience.

Training credentials, certificates and employment terms are governed by the respective provider or employer. Users should review qualifications, recognition and engagement terms before proceeding.

Support Across the Lifecycle

Repairs, maintenance and technical support

Reliable maintenance is essential for the safe and effective operation of drones. GarudHub helps customers identify participating repair centers, technicians and maintenance professionals for different types of drones and components — inspection, diagnosis, routine servicing, firmware assistance, calibration and preventive maintenance.

Service quality, warranties, timelines and charges remain the responsibility of the individual service provider.

Used Drones, Scrap & Reuse

Responsible reuse, start to finish

A drone may be upgraded, resold, refurbished, dismantled for usable parts or responsibly recycled when it reaches the end of its useful life. GarudHub facilitates connections relating to pre-owned drones, damaged equipment, reusable components and drone scrap.

Responsible handling is particularly important for electronic parts and batteries — sellers and buyers should follow all applicable safety and e-waste requirements.

Access and Protection

Loan and financing assistance

Professional and commercial drones may require a significant investment, particularly for farmers, startups, service providers and small businesses. GarudHub aims to make drone adoption more accessible by connecting eligible customers with banks, financial institutions, non-banking financial companies or other authorized finance partners.

GarudHub does not guarantee loan approval, interest rates, repayment terms or disbursal — every application is assessed independently by the respective financial institution.

Drone Insurance Support

Managing risk, responsibly

Drones may be exposed to accidental damage, theft, operational risks, third-party liability and other unexpected events. GarudHub aims to connect customers with participating insurance providers or authorized intermediaries offering drone-related insurance solutions.

All policies are issued and administered by the respective insurer. GarudHub does not act as an insurer or guarantee that a claim will be approved.

Help & FAQ

Common questions about GarudHub

What people usually ask before using the platform.

Is GarudHub a drone manufacturer or seller?

No. GarudHub is a marketplace that connects drone manufacturers, vendors, sellers, service providers, training institutes and customers — we do not manufacture or sell drones ourselves.

Does GarudHub guarantee loan approval or insurance claims?

No. Loan applications are assessed independently by the respective financial institution, and insurance policies are issued and administered by the respective insurer. GarudHub does not guarantee approval, terms or claim settlement.

Who is responsible for product pricing and warranties?

Product availability, specifications, warranties, pricing and delivery conditions are determined by the respective sellers and may vary across listings.

Are training certificates recognized by GarudHub?

Training credentials and certificates are governed by the respective training provider. Users should review qualifications and recognition before enrolling.

Do I need registration or certification to fly a drone?

Yes. Drone ownership and operation must comply with applicable aviation, safety, privacy and local regulations, and users are responsible for obtaining any required registration, certification or authorization.
