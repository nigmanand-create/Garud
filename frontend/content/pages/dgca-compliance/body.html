Compliance shouldn't feel like a maze

Every drone in India needs to be registered and flown within DGCA's Drone Rules — but the process spans multiple portals, document formats and category-specific requirements that change often.

DroneHub brings registration information, pilot requirements, document tracking and renewal reminders into a single, always-current view — so you always know what's needed and what's next.

What every drone owner needs to know

From identifying your drone's category to getting a Unique Identification Number — the essentials, laid out clearly.

Unique ID (UIN)

Every drone, regardless of category, must be registered and assigned a Unique Identification Number before it can legally fly.

Drone Categories

Nano, Micro, Small, Medium and Large — your registration path and requirements differ based on the weight category of your drone.

Type Certification

Drones above Nano category typically require type certification before they're eligible for registration.

Owner Verification

Identity and address verification of the drone owner is required as part of the registration submission.

Airspace Zones

Understand Red, Yellow and Green zones before you fly — permissions differ significantly by airspace classification.

Fees & Renewal

Remote Pilot Requirements

Who can legally fly a drone

Beyond registering the drone itself, the person operating it needs to meet certification requirements for most drone categories.

Minimum Age

Remote pilots must meet the minimum age requirement set under the applicable Drone Rules.

Certified Training

Completion of a Remote Pilot Certificate course from a DGCA-approved training organisation.

Medical Fitness

Basic medical fitness declaration is required for certain drone categories and operation types.

Practical Assessment

A hands-on flying assessment conducted by the training institute as part of certification.

Remote Pilot Certificate

Issued on successful completion — required to legally operate drones above the Nano category.

Periodic Renewal

Certificates are valid for a defined period and must be renewed before expiry to remain compliant.

Help & FAQ

Common compliance questions

Quick answers to what most drone owners ask before registering.

Do I need to register a toy or Nano drone?

Nano drones (under 250g) have relaxed requirements compared to higher categories, but identification and basic operating rules still apply. Confirm your drone's exact category before assuming it's exempt.

How long does registration typically take?

Timelines vary based on document completeness and category. Applications with all required documents correctly submitted move through review faster.

What happens if my Remote Pilot Certificate expires?

Flying with an expired certificate is non-compliant. Renewal should be initiated before expiry — this platform sends reminders ahead of the deadline.

Can I fly in any location once my drone is registered?

No. Registration alone doesn't grant flight permission everywhere. Airspace zone (Red/Yellow/Green) and any required digital permission still apply per flight location.

Where do I submit my actual registration application?

Official applications are submitted through DGCA's Digital Sky platform. This page helps you prepare and track the process, not replace the official submission.
