Help & FAQ

Common questions

What most applicants ask before applying to a government scheme or tender.

Are these schemes and tenders official government listings?

They're compiled from official sources for your convenience, but this platform is not a government portal — always verify details on the official department or tender site before applying.

Can individuals apply, or only registered businesses?

Eligibility varies by scheme — some are open to individuals, others require business or cooperative registration. Check the eligibility criteria for each listing.

How do I know if something is official government information?

Every listing on this page is clearly marked as platform-compiled guidance. For anything binding — eligibility, submission and final decisions — we link out to the official department or tender portal directly.

Do you help with the actual application process?

Guided support can help you understand requirements and prepare documentation, but final submission is always done through the official portal.

How often is this information updated?

Listings are reviewed and updated regularly, but always cross-check time-sensitive details against the official source before applying.
