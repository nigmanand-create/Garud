Help & FAQ

Common questions from enterprise teams

What most procurement and operations teams ask before engaging us.

Do you offer both one-time procurement and ongoing managed services?

Yes — you can procure a one-time survey/deployment, or set up an ongoing managed service with recurring flights and reporting, depending on what your sites need.

Can the same program cover multiple sites or industries?

Yes, deployments can be scaled and standardised across multiple sites, even across different industry verticals within the same organisation.

What kind of reporting do we receive after a flight?

Reports are tailored to your industry — progress imagery, volumetric data, thermal scans or compliance documentation, delivered in a format your team can act on.

Do you handle the required flight permissions and compliance?

Yes, flight planning and applicable regulatory compliance are handled as part of the deployment process for every site.
