Help & FAQ

Common repair questions

What most drone owners ask before booking a repair.

How is the repair estimate calculated?

After inspection, the service centre provides a cost estimate based on parts and labour required. Nothing is repaired without your approval on the estimate.

Is pickup and drop available everywhere?

Availability depends on your location and the assigned service centre. This is confirmed at the time of booking your repair request.

What if my drone is still under warranty?

If your drone's warranty is active and the issue is covered, the applicable warranty is applied automatically and reflected in your service history.

Can I track repair status in real time?

Yes — every repair moves through Request, Assigned, In Progress and Completed stages, visible to you throughout.

Do I need to create a drone profile before booking a repair?

It speeds things up considerably — a saved drone profile means the service centre already has your model, serial and warranty details on hand.
