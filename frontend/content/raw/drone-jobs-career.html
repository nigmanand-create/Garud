Help & FAQ

Common career questions

What most job seekers ask before applying.

Do I need a Remote Pilot Certificate to apply for pilot roles?

Most piloting roles require a valid Remote Pilot Certificate. Some entry-level or trainee positions may accept candidates currently completing certification.

Can I apply for freelance or contract work only?

Yes — your job profile lets you specify availability, including freelance and contract-only preferences.

How do employers find my profile?

Employers search by skills, certification, location and availability — a complete profile significantly improves how often you're shortlisted.

Are non-piloting roles like technicians also listed here?

Yes — technician, survey, training and enterprise operations roles are all part of the same job listings.

Is there a fee to create a job profile?

Creating and maintaining a job profile is free for job seekers on the platform.
