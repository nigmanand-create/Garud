Help & FAQ

Common buying questions

What most first-time buyers ask before ordering.

Can I get a custom quote for bulk orders?

Yes — enterprise and bulk orders go through the procurement flow, where you'll receive quotations from multiple verified sellers.

Does the listed price include accessories?

Base prices typically cover the drone unit only. Accessories like batteries, propellers and cases are usually quoted separately.

Is warranty handled by the seller or DroneHub?

Warranty is honoured by the manufacturer or seller as per their terms; DroneHub verifies this is disclosed upfront before you order.

Can I compare drones across categories?

Yes — the comparison table lets you view specs across commercial, agriculture, survey, camera and enterprise categories side by side.

Do you offer financing or EMI options?

Availability depends on the seller. Ask about financing options directly when you request a quote.
