Help & FAQ

Common training questions

What most first-time trainees ask before enrolling.

Can I complete training fully online?

Theory modules can often be completed online, but the practical flying assessment must be done in person at an approved facility.

How do I know if an institute is genuinely DGCA-approved?

Institutes listed here are checked for approval status before being added. You can also verify directly with DGCA if you'd like independent confirmation.

What happens after I complete my practical assessment?

The institute issues a training completion certificate, which feeds into your Remote Pilot Certificate application.

Can I switch institutes mid-course?

This depends on the institute's own policy and course structure. Check transfer terms before enrolling if this matters to you.

Do I need my own drone to train?

No — most institutes provide training drones for the practical portion of the course as part of the fee.
