Help & FAQ

Common loan & insurance questions

What most drone owners ask before applying.

Who is eligible for a drone loan?

Individuals, farmers, small businesses and enterprises can apply. Eligibility and terms depend on the lending partner and the intended use of the drone.

What does drone insurance typically cover?

Most plans cover accidental damage, crashes, theft and third-party liability. Enterprise fleet plans can bundle multiple drones under one policy.

How long does loan approval take?

Once your documents are verified, approval timelines depend on the lending partner — most applications are reviewed within a few working days.

Can I apply for both a loan and insurance together?

Yes — many buyers finance the purchase and insure the same drone in one enquiry. You'll be matched to lending and insurance partners separately.

How do I file an insurance claim?

Raise a claim through your policy provider with incident details and documentation. Our team can assist with coordination if you get stuck.
