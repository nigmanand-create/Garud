Help & FAQ

Common questions from international organizations

What most companies ask before starting their India journey.

Does GarudHub guarantee regulatory approval or business registration?

No. Regulatory approvals and statutory permissions remain subject to the applicable Indian authorities and their prevailing requirements. GarudHub provides guidance and coordination, not approvals.

Can GarudHub help with manufacturing in India?

Yes — we can support exploration of Indian manufacturing opportunities, OEM partnerships, component ecosystem and distribution networks.

Do you only work with drone manufacturers?

No — our support extends to technology companies, investors, training organizations, UAV service companies, GIS companies, agri-tech companies and research institutions as well.

How long does the India market-entry process typically take?

Timelines vary based on your objectives — market assessment and strategy can begin quickly, while business setup and regulatory compliance depend on your specific business model and applicable requirements.
