Help & FAQ

Common partnership questions

What most manufacturers and service providers ask before joining.

Who can become a GarudHub partner?

Drone manufacturers, OEMs, payload manufacturers, drone service providers, maintenance companies, training organizations, technology companies, GIS & mapping companies, agriculture technology companies and research/innovation organizations.

Is there a cost to list as a partner?

Our team will share the applicable registration details for your category during onboarding — reach out through the form above for current terms.

How long does profile verification take?

Most applications are reviewed and verified within a few business days, depending on the completeness of the submitted documentation.

Can I update my product catalogue after listing?

Yes — partners can update their profile, products and service network on an ongoing basis.

Do you work with training institutes as well as manufacturers?

Yes — GarudHub connects training organizations with learners just as it connects manufacturers with buyers, all within the same ecosystem.
